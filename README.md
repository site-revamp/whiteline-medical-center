# Whiteline Medical Center – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Whiteline Medical Center LLC is a dental healthcare provider located in Al Mizhar 1, Dubai, offering general, cosmetic, restorative and specialist dental treatments for individuals and families.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting the clinic as a trusted, patient-focused dental care provider. The direction prioritises credibility, accessible appointment enquiries and stronger visibility for the dental team and available treatments.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Fixed Broken Credibility Statistics
- Addressed incorrectly displayed homepage counters, including "0 K+" and "0 +".
- Removed reliance on broken statistics as credibility indicators.
- Improved the presentation of clinic information without introducing unverified figures.

**Problem addressed:** Incorrect counters made the homepage appear unfinished and could undermine visitor confidence in the clinic.

### 2. Improved Appointment Conversion Journey
- Made appointment enquiry actions more prominent.
- Strengthened the visibility of contact options, including telephone and appointment booking pathways.
- Improved the placement and consistency of calls to action throughout the homepage.

**Problem addressed:** The previous website did not provide a sufficiently prominent or consistent journey from treatment discovery to appointment enquiry.

### 3. Stronger Doctor-Led Trust Building
- Improved the presentation of the dental team.
- Gave greater visibility to the identified practitioners and their respective specialisms.
- Structured doctor information to help visitors understand the clinic's available expertise.

**Problem addressed:** The previous doctor presentation did not sufficiently communicate practitioners' professional roles and specialisms, limiting opportunities to build patient confidence.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Created a more polished and professional dental healthcare appearance.
- **Layout:** Improved the hierarchy of homepage content, treatment information and doctor profiles.
- **User Experience:** Made contact and appointment enquiry pathways easier to identify.
- **Content Clarity:** Improved the presentation of dental services and practitioner information.
- **Consistency:** Established clearer placement and presentation of calls to action across the homepage.
- **Credibility:** Avoided displaying unreliable statistics and prioritised identifiable clinic and practitioner information.

## Project Objective

The redesigned homepage aims to strengthen Whiteline Medical Center's online credibility, improve the visibility of its dental team and treatments, and make it easier for prospective patients to enquire about appointments.
